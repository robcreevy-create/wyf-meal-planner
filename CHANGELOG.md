# WYF Version 1.2

## Added
- Robbie/Rachel device-specific welcome greeting with Switch name
- Comfort as a fourth meal style
- Dynamic maximum of two meat species for 3–4 dinners and three for 5–7 dinners
- Per-recipe image mapping with fallback to illustrated food symbols
- Dynamic meat-limit explanation on the planning form

## Improved
- Single-meal swapping preserves all unchanged meals and never exceeds the meat limit
- More species variety when multiple meat types are selected
- Saved meat preferences are restored on reopening
- Shopping quantities do not include stale child portions when children are set to zero

## Preserved
- 38 recipes, original ingredient measures and detailed cooking steps
- Existing WYF app icon and public GitHub Pages route
- Local plans, favourites, adult and child settings, household extras and their history, and shopping checkmarks
- Fish-risk filtering, print/PDF and iPhone sharing

## Not yet included
- Individual food photos for every meal (mapping is ready, and placeholders remain until photos are added)
- Shared real-time shopping sync between iPhones
- Automatic generation of new AI recipes within the standalone app
