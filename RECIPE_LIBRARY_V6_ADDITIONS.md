# WYF Recipe Library 6: additions and cooking clarifications

**Six new recipes: three Comfort and three Healthy. Two-adult base quantities.**

This is an additions document, not a replacement for the existing 54-recipe master. The accompanying app module keeps those 54 recipes and adds these six, giving 60. It does not automatically edit project sources or the live GitHub app.

These are original WYF recipes with complete planned quantities and methods. They have not been kitchen-tested; preparation and cooking times are estimates. The Healthy label follows the project convention of leaner protein, plenty of vegetables, pulses or wholegrain, and measured fat. No calorie or nutrition analysis is claimed.

## Cooking-instruction changes

- State the heat when heating a frying pan. Give a specific destination when reducing the heat. Preserve existing explicit settings.
- When meat is only browned, say to lift it onto a plate and set aside while continuing the recipe. Explain that it is not yet fully cooked and finishes later in the same cooking session. Do not confuse that with removing fully cooked meat to keep warm.
- In Lemon Chicken & Vegetable Orzo, the orzo simmers for 5 minutes before the chicken returns, then for another 5–7 minutes. Total orzo cooking is 10–12 minutes, not 10–12 minutes plus an extra stage. The two timing instructions share one step.

### Corrected orzo step, base quantities for two adults

Stir in the orzo and pour over 450 ml boiling water with the stock cube dissolved. Bring to a gentle bubble over medium heat, then reduce the heat to low. Simmer over low heat for 5 minutes, stirring often. Return the chicken and any juices to the pan, then simmer over low heat for a further 5–7 minutes, until the orzo is tender and the chicken reaches 75°C. That is 10–12 minutes of orzo cooking in total. Add a small splash of boiling water if the pan dries out before the orzo is tender.

## Portions and safety

Multiply food quantities by (adults + parent-selected child portion fractions) ÷ 2. Do not multiply cooking times, oven temperatures, chopping sizes or per-piece thicknesses. Use suitably larger cookware or cook in batches. For a fractional egg, beat an egg and measure the fraction needed. Pantry salt and pepper are measured starting allowances; adjust to taste. Water for boiling is tap water, not a purchase suggestion.

Cook poultry, pork, minced meat and sausages to at least 75°C at the centre. Check with a clean probe rather than relying on timing or colour. Keep raw meat and its juices separate from ready-to-eat food and wash hands, boards and utensils after handling raw meat. Check every purchased product label for allergens.

Food-safety source: Safefood, “How to check your meat is cooked”, https://www.safefood.net/food-safety/check-meat-cooked (consulted 15 November 2026).

## 55. Chicken, Leek & Pea Puff-Pastry Pie

with carrots and a creamy mustard sauce

**Serves:** 2 adults · **Style:** Comfort · **Meat:** chicken · **Estimated time:** 50–60 min · **App ID:** WYF37

### Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Chicken breast | 300 g |
| Leek, trimmed | 200 g |
| Carrots | 150 g |
| Frozen peas | 120 g |
| Ready-rolled puff pastry | 160 g |
| Semi-skimmed milk | 210 ml |
| Butter | 20 g |

### Pantry

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Olive oil | 5 ml |
| Plain flour | 20 g |
| Dijon mustard | 10 g |
| Vegetarian stock powder | 3 g |
| Water for the sauce | 150 ml |
| Dried thyme | 0.5 tsp |
| Salt | 0.5 g (Adjust to taste; stock and mustard contain salt.) |
| Black pepper | 0.25 g |

### Cooking instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Cut the chicken into 2 cm pieces. Wash the leek carefully and slice it thinly. Dice the carrots into small 5 mm pieces. Set out a shallow ovenproof dish, roughly 20 cm across, and a large frying pan.
2. Heat 5 ml olive oil in the frying pan over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and brown for 4–5 minutes, turning occasionally. Lift it onto a plate and set aside while you continue cooking; it is not fully cooked yet and will finish cooking in the sauce.
3. Reduce the heat to medium. Add the butter, leek and carrots to the same pan. Cook for 6–8 minutes, stirring, until the leek is soft. Stir in the flour and thyme and cook for 1 minute.
4. Measure out 10 ml of the milk and reserve it for brushing the pastry. Gradually stir the remaining 200 ml milk into the vegetables. Dissolve the stock powder in 150 ml hot water and stir it in with the mustard, salt and pepper. Keep the pan over medium heat until the sauce starts bubbling.
5. Return the chicken and its juices to the pan. Reduce the heat to low and simmer gently for 8–10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chicken reaches 75°C and the carrots are tender. Stir in the frozen peas and cook over low heat for 2 minutes. Transfer the hot filling to the ovenproof dish.
6. Lay the puff pastry over the filling, trimming to fit the dish. Press the edges down and cut a small steam vent in the centre. Brush with the reserved 10 ml milk. Bake for 20–25 minutes until the pastry is risen, golden and cooked through and the filling is piping hot. Rest for 3 minutes, then divide between plates.

### Allergens

- Wheat/gluten: flour and puff pastry.
- Milk: butter and milk; pastry may also contain milk.
- Mustard: Dijon mustard.
- Stock powder may contain celery. Check pastry, mustard and stock labels.

## 56. Beef & Aubergine Moussaka

with a yoghurt and cheese topping

**Serves:** 2 adults · **Style:** Comfort · **Meat:** beef · **Estimated time:** 55–65 min · **App ID:** WYF38

### Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Lean beef mince | 250 g |
| Aubergine | 400 g |
| Onion | 150 g |
| Garlic | 2 cloves |
| Chopped tomatoes | 400 g |
| Greek yoghurt | 150 g |
| Egg | 1 |
| Italian hard cheese | 30 g |

### Pantry

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Olive oil | 15 ml |
| Tomato purée | 20 g |
| Dried oregano | 1 tsp |
| Ground cinnamon | 0.25 tsp |
| Salt | 0.5 g |
| Black pepper | 0.25 g |

### Cooking instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Slice the aubergine into 8 mm rounds. Spread on a lined baking tray, brush both sides with 10 ml olive oil and season with half the salt. Roast for 20–25 minutes, turning after 12 minutes, until softened and lightly golden. Meanwhile, dice the onion and crush the garlic.
2. Heat the remaining 5 ml olive oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Brown the beef mince for 5–6 minutes, breaking it into small pieces with a spoon. Reduce the heat to medium, add the onion and cook for 5 minutes. Add the garlic, oregano, cinnamon and tomato purée and cook for 1 minute.
3. Stir in the chopped tomatoes, remaining salt and black pepper. Bring to a gentle bubble over medium heat, then reduce the heat to low and simmer uncovered for 10–12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until thickened and the beef reaches 75°C. Remove the pan from the heat.
4. In a bowl, beat the egg until smooth, then mix it with the Greek yoghurt and half the grated cheese. Use the beaten-egg fraction shown in the ingredients if preparing a scaled recipe; do not round up to a whole extra egg.
5. Reduce the oven to 210°C (190°C fan). In a shallow ovenproof dish, roughly 20 cm across, layer half the aubergine, all the beef sauce and the remaining aubergine. Spoon over the yoghurt mixture and sprinkle with the remaining cheese.
6. Bake for 20–25 minutes until the topping is set, lightly browned and the centre is piping hot, at least 75°C. Leave to stand for 5 minutes before dividing between plates.

### Allergens

- Milk: Greek yoghurt and hard cheese.
- Egg: topping; some hard cheeses also contain egg-derived lysozyme. Check the cheese label.

## 57. Sausage, Broccoli & Tomato Gnocchi Bake

with a golden mozzarella topping

**Serves:** 2 adults · **Style:** Comfort · **Meat:** pork · **Estimated time:** 35–45 min · **App ID:** WYF39

### Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Pork sausages | 250 g |
| Potato gnocchi | 350 g |
| Broccoli | 250 g |
| Cherry tomatoes | 200 g |
| Passata | 250 g |
| Mozzarella | 80 g |
| Garlic | 2 cloves |

### Pantry

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Olive oil | 5 ml |
| Water for the sauce | 100 ml |
| Dried oregano | 1 tsp |
| Salt | 0.5 g (Adjust to taste; sausages can be salty.) |
| Black pepper | 0.25 g |

### Cooking instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Cut the broccoli into small 2 cm florets and finely slice any tender stem. Halve the tomatoes and crush the garlic. Squeeze the sausages out of their skins and break the meat into small 2 cm pieces. Wash your hands and utensils after handling the raw sausages.
2. Heat the olive oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Fry the sausage pieces for 6–8 minutes, stirring and turning until browned. Reduce the heat to medium, add the garlic and oregano, and cook for 30 seconds.
3. Add the tomatoes, passata, 100 ml water, salt and pepper. Bring to a gentle bubble over medium heat. Stir in the broccoli, reduce the heat to low, cover and simmer for 5 minutes. Stir halfway through.
4. Stir the uncooked potato gnocchi into the sauce. Cover and simmer over low heat for 3–5 minutes, stirring halfway through, until the gnocchi are tender, the broccoli is almost tender and the sausage meat reaches 75°C. Follow the gnocchi packet if it requires a longer cooking time.
5. Transfer everything to an ovenproof dish, or leave in the pan only if it is oven-safe. Tear the mozzarella over the top. Bake for 10–12 minutes until the cheese is melted and golden and the sauce is bubbling. Rest for 2 minutes before serving.

### Allergens

- Milk: mozzarella.
- Wheat/gluten is commonly present in gnocchi and sausages. Gnocchi may also contain egg.
- Sausages may contain sulphites, soya or mustard. Check the purchased labels.

## 58. Pork Medallions with Apple & Lentils

with green beans and a lemon-mustard dressing

**Serves:** 2 adults · **Style:** Healthy · **Meat:** pork · **Estimated time:** 30–35 min · **App ID:** WYF40

### Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Pork tenderloin | 300 g |
| Cooked lentils, drained | 240 g |
| Apple, cored | 150 g |
| Green beans | 200 g |
| Carrots | 100 g |
| Onion | 100 g |
| Garlic | 1 clove |
| Lemon | 0.5 |
| Fresh parsley | 5 g |

### Pantry

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Olive oil | 10 ml |
| Dijon mustard | 10 g |
| Vegetarian stock powder | 2 g |
| Water for the lentils | 100 ml |
| Water for boiling beans | 750 ml (Tap water; discard after cooking.) |
| Salt | 0.5 g |
| Black pepper | 0.25 g |

### Cooking instructions

1. Cut the pork tenderloin into 2 cm-thick medallions. Dice the onion and carrots into small 5 mm pieces, crush the garlic, and cut the cored apple into 1 cm pieces. Trim the green beans, drain and rinse the lentils, and chop the parsley. Keep the pork and its juices separate from the prepared vegetables.
2. Heat 5 ml olive oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Cook the onion and carrots for 6–8 minutes, stirring, until starting to soften. Add the apple and garlic and cook for 2 minutes.
3. Dissolve the stock powder in 100 ml hot water. Stir it into the vegetables with the lentils, mustard, half the parsley and half the salt and pepper. Bring to a gentle bubble over medium heat, then reduce the heat to low. Simmer for 6–8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the carrots are tender and the lentils are piping hot. Keep warm over the lowest heat while finishing the pork.
4. While the lentils simmer, bring 750 ml water to the boil in a saucepan over high heat. Add the green beans, reduce the heat to medium and simmer for 4–5 minutes until tender. Drain.
5. Season the pork with the remaining salt and pepper. Heat the remaining 5 ml olive oil in a second frying pan over medium-high heat. Cook the medallions for 3–4 minutes on each side, then check the centre reaches 75°C. Continue over medium heat if needed. Transfer the fully cooked pork to a clean plate and rest for 3 minutes.
6. Remove the lentils from the heat and stir in the lemon juice and remaining parsley. Divide the lentils and green beans between plates, then add the rested pork and any resting juices. Serve immediately.

### Allergens

- Mustard: Dijon mustard.
- Stock powder may contain celery; check the label.

## 59. Lemon-Herb Turkey & Bulgur Stuffed Peppers

with spinach, courgette and lemon yoghurt

**Serves:** 2 adults · **Style:** Healthy · **Meat:** turkey · **Estimated time:** 40–45 min · **App ID:** WYF41

### Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Turkey mince | 250 g |
| Large bell peppers | 2 (Large peppers, about 200 g each; halve lengthways.) |
| Dry bulgur wheat | 80 g |
| Courgette | 150 g |
| Onion | 100 g |
| Garlic | 2 cloves |
| Baby spinach | 80 g |
| Lemon | 1 |
| Greek yoghurt | 80 g |
| Fresh parsley | 5 g |

### Pantry

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Olive oil | 10 ml |
| Dried oregano | 1 tsp |
| Vegetarian stock powder | 2 g |
| Water for the bulgur | 160 ml |
| Salt | 0.5 g |
| Black pepper | 0.25 g |

### Cooking instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 220°C (200°C fan). Halve the peppers lengthways and remove the seeds. Place cut-side up in an ovenproof dish, brush with 5 ml olive oil and roast for 15–18 minutes. Meanwhile, finely dice the onion and courgette, crush the garlic, chop the parsley and zest the lemon.
2. Put the bulgur, stock powder and 160 ml water into a small saucepan. Bring to the boil over high heat, reduce the heat to low, cover and simmer for 10–12 minutes, or according to the packet. The bulgur should be tender and the liquid absorbed. Remove from the heat and leave covered for 5 minutes. Add a little extra boiling water only if the packet requires it.
3. While the peppers and bulgur cook, heat the remaining 5 ml olive oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Cook the turkey mince for 5–6 minutes, breaking it into small pieces. Reduce the heat to medium, add the onion and courgette and cook for 5–6 minutes. Stir in the garlic and oregano and cook for 1 minute.
4. Add the spinach to the frying pan and stir over medium heat for 1–2 minutes until wilted. Check that the turkey reaches 75°C. Remove the pan from the heat, then stir in the cooked bulgur, lemon zest, juice of half the lemon, half the parsley, salt and pepper.
5. Spoon the filling into the roasted pepper halves, mounding it gently. Return the dish to the oven for 8–10 minutes, until the peppers are tender and the filling is piping hot. Spread any extra filling around the peppers in the dish so nothing is wasted.
6. Mix the yoghurt with the juice of the remaining half lemon and the remaining parsley. Divide the stuffed peppers between plates and spoon over the lemon yoghurt.

### Allergens

- Wheat/gluten: bulgur.
- Milk: Greek yoghurt.
- Stock powder may contain celery; check the label.

## 60. Lemon Chicken & Butter Bean Skillet

with courgette, cherry tomatoes and spinach

**Serves:** 2 adults · **Style:** Healthy · **Meat:** chicken · **Estimated time:** 30–35 min · **App ID:** WYF42

### Ingredients

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Chicken breast | 300 g |
| Cooked butter beans, drained | 240 g |
| Cherry tomatoes | 200 g |
| Courgette | 200 g |
| Baby spinach | 100 g |
| Onion | 100 g |
| Garlic | 2 cloves |
| Lemon | 1 |
| Fresh parsley | 5 g |

### Pantry

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Olive oil | 10 ml |
| Dried oregano | 1 tsp |
| Vegetarian stock powder | 3 g |
| Water for the sauce | 150 ml |
| Salt | 0.5 g |
| Black pepper | 0.25 g |

### Cooking instructions

1. Cut the chicken into 2 cm pieces. Dice the onion, cut the courgette into 1 cm cubes, halve the tomatoes, crush the garlic and chop the parsley. Zest the lemon and drain and rinse the butter beans. Keep raw chicken and its juices away from the prepared vegetables.
2. Heat 5 ml olive oil in a large deep frying pan over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and brown for 4–5 minutes, turning occasionally. Lift onto a plate and set aside while you continue cooking; it is not fully cooked yet and will finish cooking in the sauce.
3. Reduce the heat to medium. Add the remaining 5 ml olive oil, onion and courgette to the same pan and cook for 5–6 minutes, stirring. Add the garlic and oregano and cook for 1 minute.
4. Dissolve the stock powder in 150 ml hot water. Add it to the pan with the tomatoes, butter beans, lemon zest, salt and pepper. Return the chicken and its juices. Bring to a gentle bubble over medium heat, then reduce the heat to low and simmer for 8–10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chicken reaches 75°C and the courgette is tender.
5. Stir in the spinach and cook over low heat for 1–2 minutes until wilted. Remove from the heat and add the juice of half the lemon and the parsley. Divide between bowls and serve with the remaining lemon cut into wedges.

### Allergens

- Stock powder may contain celery or other allergens. Check the purchased label.
- No dairy, egg or wheat ingredients are specified, but this is not an allergen-free guarantee.
